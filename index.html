Welcome

Search any chemical by name, CAS number, formula or PubChem CID. Data is fetched live from PubChem; every safety field shows its source and verification label.

Green Alternatives

Type the chemical you want to replace (matching chemicals appear as you type) and what you need it for. The system suggests lower-hazard substitutes that suit that purpose and ranks them with live PubChem GHS hazard data. Suggestions come from a curated list of well-known substitutions plus class-level rules (no open database maps substitutes for every chemical, so none is invented). Technical suitability must always be confirmed separately.

Chemical Storage Planner

Groups come from each chemical's GHS codes. Pair checks use the curated sample rules, so "none found" never means compatible.

Safety & Emergency Guide

For any spill, leak, fire, exposure or accidental mixing that is large, unknown, or causes symptoms: leave the area, warn others, and call your local emergency number or trained response team. Do not attempt cleanup, neutralisation or rescue without training.

Chemical Compatibility

Type any number of chemicals (up to 10) separated by commas, e.g. acetone, bleach, ammonia, vinegar, sodium hydroxide. You get a pair-by-pair matrix, the reason for each result, where to store each one and a final recommendation. Safety screening only, not permission to mix.
